About this tool

MindPulse sends your answers to a Random Forest regression model running on a FastAPI server. The model uses your age, gender, country group, academic level, platform, purpose of use, usage hours, unlocks, study, activity and sleep hours, and stress level.

Disclaimer: MindPulse is an educational project. Results are not a diagnosis or medical advice. If you are struggling, please contact a qualified professional or a local helpline.
